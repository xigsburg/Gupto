## 🕶️ About Gupto

<table>
<tr>
<td>

*"Gupto"* (গুপ্ত) means **secret** in Bengali — and that's the whole idea baked into one word.

Gupto is **Bangladesh's premium random chat platform**, built for people who crave real, spontaneous human connection *without* handing over their identity to get it.

🎥 Video chat. 💬 Text chat. ⚽ Live football watch-parties. 🏠 Fully custom rooms.
All wrapped in a platform that collects **no account data beyond what's necessary**, keeps **zero chat history**, and stays **encrypted, always**.

No profile to build. No feed to scroll. No trail left behind.
**Just you, a stranger, and a conversation that vanishes the moment it ends.**

</td>
</tr>

## 🪙 The Token System

<div align="center">

Gupto runs on a **fair-access token system** — instead of ads, paywalls, or invasive data collection, tokens keep the platform balanced and abuse-resistant while staying open to everyone.

| 🪙 Principle | Why It Matters |
|:---:|---|
| **Fair Access** | No one can flood or spam the matching queue |
| **No Data Trade-off** | You spend tokens, not your privacy |
| **Everyone Starts Equal** | Simple, transparent, credit-based access |

</div>
